Layout rules

Hand-drawn look, ruler-straight layout.

one left edge one right edge 16 80 8 288 8 16 in 24 8 36 44 28

1 · Grid

  • Everything snaps to 4px. Spacing only uses 4, 8, 12, 16 and 24.
  • Margin 16 · rail 80 · gap 8 · panel 288. Fireworks panel 464.
  • The wallet is pinned top-left and never moves, panel open or shut.

2 · Edges

  • One left edge: panel content starts 16 in. No indents.
  • One right edge: prices, values, toggles and close end 16 in.
  • Cards: 10 padding, 36 icon slot, 12 gutter, so names line up from card to card.
  • Card actions span the full card width.

3 · Rhythm

  • 24 between sections. 8 between items. 8 under a title.
  • 12 between a title and the tabs that belong to it.
  • Anything you press is 44 tall. Bars are 28 in cards and 14 inline.
  • Short panels hug their content. Only full lists run to the bottom.

4 · Type

  • Chewy for titles (28), sections (20) and numbers.
  • Shantell Sans for the rest: names 15, details 13, rail 11.
  • Numbers sit right-aligned, with the currency icon in front.

5 · Shape

  • A sticker is wobbly corners, a 2px ink line and a hard 3px shadow. No blur.
  • Only floating things tilt: toasts, tips, the polaroid, the active tab and the selected tile. Max 2°.
  • Columns stay straight.

6 · Words

  • Labels are 1–2 words. Show the number, not a sentence.
  • Never explain the game.
  • One joke per screen at most. “Whoosh!” counts.

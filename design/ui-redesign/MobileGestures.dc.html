Touch map

One thumb. The sky stays playable.

1 2 3 4 5 6 8
  1. 1 Tap the sky. Launch a firework, grab sparkles.
  2. 2 Tap a tab. Its sheet opens halfway. Tap again to close.
  3. 3 Drag the handle. Half, full or gone. It follows your thumb.
  4. 4 Swipe the sheet sideways. Next tab. In the studio, swipe the panel for the next step.
  5. 5 Swipe the tab bar down. Everything tucks away. Tap the bubble to bring it back.
  6. 6 Swipe the tab bar up. Reopens your last tab.
  7. 7 Hold to confirm. Resets fill up over 1.2s, so no accidents.
  8. 8 Drag the sky sideways. Pan along the show.

For dev

  • Tabs, buy buttons and studio actions live in the bottom half. Targets are 44px or more (buy buttons 48px).
  • Sheets follow the finger 1:1 and snap with a spring: 360ms, slight overshoot. Detents: half 470 (Upgrades, Buildings, Crowd), half 404 (More), full 112.
  • Upgrades, Buildings and Crowd pan the camera up 240px, so the ground, fans and launchers sit just above the sheet. Launch, and Locate from a full sheet, dip the sheet for about 2s.
  • Haptics: light tick on buy and on snap, a success buzz on unlocks.
  • Wallet under the notch, tab bar above the home bar. The studio preview is the real engine canvas, same as desktop.
